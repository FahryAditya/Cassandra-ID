Exploring AI in Anime Production: Latent Frame Synthesis & Keyframe Interpolation

group Cassandra Research Team & AI Study Group • calendar_today September 2026 • menu_book 34 pages

This study examines the implementation of hybrid conditional diffusion architectures within traditional 2D animation pipelines. By analyzing keyframe consistency across 1,200 production cuts, we demonstrate an 18.4% acceleration in in-betweening workflows while strictly retaining signature animator line weights, color balance, and artistic idiosyncrasies without artifact regression.
